Privacy notice

Site Kept is run by Ibra, Ibrahim Al-Roudhan trading as Site Kept, London, United Kingdom (full postal address on request and in every email). Contact: hello@sitekept.co.uk. I am the data controller for the information below.

What I collect and why

Business contact details for outreach. I find UK limited companies whose public website looks out of date, and I email the business address they publish (for example info@ or a named business contact listed on their website or Companies House). I keep: company name, company number, website, the published email address, notes on the website, and whether you replied or opted out. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (offering a relevant service to a business, at its business address, with an easy opt-out). I only email companies and LLPs, never sole traders, partnerships or personal addresses.

Messages you send me. If you use the contact form or reply to an email, I keep your name, email, website and message so I can answer you and, if you become a client, deliver the service. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, then contract.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. The host keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, page) for security for a short period.

How long I keep it

Outreach details of businesses that don't reply are deleted 90 days after the last email. If you opt out, I keep only your email address (or a one-way hash of it) on a suppression list so I never contact you again. Client records are kept for as long as you are a client and 6 years after, for tax.

Who else sees it

Only the services that make this work: the email provider that sends and stores my mail, the website host, and the form endpoint that passes contact-form messages to my inbox. I don't sell or share your details with anyone else. Some of these providers may store data outside the UK under standard safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what I hold, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to being contacted. The fastest way to stop emails is the unsubscribe page or the link in any email, which takes effect immediately. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.
