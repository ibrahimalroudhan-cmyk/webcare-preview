How the uptime watch works

  1. Every 2 minutes a monitor loads your site and checks it answers, loads fast enough and still shows your real content.
  2. If it fails twice in a row my phone gets a priority alert with the site, the error and a likely fix.
  3. I fix it: restore a backup, roll back an update, renew a certificate or chase the host, whatever it takes.
  4. You get a short note saying what happened and what I did, and it goes in your monthly report.

Questions

Do I have to move my hosting?

No. I work with the hosting you have. If it's slow or overpriced I'll tell you, and moving is your choice.

What if my site isn't WordPress?

Squarespace, Wix, plain HTML and most others are fine for edits, checks and refreshes. Plugin updates only apply where there are plugins.

How fast are edits?

Small edits are usually done within two working days; urgent fixes (site down, broken forms) come first.

Can I stop any time?

Yes: 30 days' notice, no exit fee, and you keep everything.
